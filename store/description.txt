A Pebble Time 2 watch face that recreates the look of the Casio W-221H: a crisp LCD with slanted 7-segment digits, faint unlit segments, a dot-matrix weekday and a BT / CHG / SIG / MUTE indicator box.

WHAT IT SHOWS
- Time (12 or 24 hour), weekday, date and a DST indicator
- Temperature or seconds
- Four bezel corners, each showing battery level, steps, distance, heart rate, about thirty weather and air quality readings (feels like, high/low, rain chance, wind, UV, sunrise, AQI and more), your own text or nothing, plus an optional WR badge
- Indicators for phone, charging, hourly chime and Quiet Time

SETTINGS
- 12/24-hour, date order, leading zeros, Celsius or Fahrenheit (or follow your watch)
- Seconds always ticking, or for a set time after a wrist shake
- Optional hourly chime with volume
- Black or silver case, inverted colors, slanted or straight digits, unlit segments, backlight color
- Vibration when your phone disconnects or reconnects

PERMISSIONS
Location (via your phone) for the weather and health data for steps and heart rate. Only a weather request to Open-Meteo leaves your phone, with your location rounded to about 100 meters.

Pebble Time 2 only.
