New: the bottom bezel text is now the same size as the top one, and the bottom strip is thinner, which gives the LCD a little more room.
New: weather refresh setting (Weather, Update every): 30 minutes by default, or 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 hours to make fewer requests.
New: UV index, wind speed, rain and humidity can be shown in any bezel corner; a setting picks metric or imperial units for wind and rain.
Fixed: the letters in the WR and HR badges are now vertically centred.
New: the Units section now holds all the unit choices (temperature, distance, wind and rain), and a kind of information (battery, steps, distance, heart rate, UV, wind, rain, humidity, WR) can only be shown in one bezel corner at a time.
New: the air quality index (AQI) can be shown in any bezel corner, on the US or the European scale (Units).
New: about thirty weather and air quality readings for the bezel corners: feels like, high and low, conditions, chance of rain, snow, dew point, cloud cover, visibility, pressure, wind gusts and direction, UV maximum, sunrise and sunset, and the air pollutants.
